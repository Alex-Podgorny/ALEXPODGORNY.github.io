Research

Current postdoctoral project

Since September 2026, I have been working at Centre Borelli with Anne Sabourin on a problem at the intersection of extreme value theory, time series, and statistical learning. The goal is to estimate a future quantile of a time series from its recent dynamics, conditionally on the occurrence of an extreme event. The project relies on the spectral tail process of regularly varying time series and on a quantile-regression procedure built from the most extreme observations. One of the main theoretical objectives is to derive non-asymptotic excess-risk bounds that account for both the complexity of the predictor class and temporal dependence. This work is ongoing.

First postdoctoral position

From October 2025 to 2026, I held a first postdoctoral position at ENSAI and IRMAR within the ANR project FunMathStat. My work focused on nonparametric tests invariant under group actions in reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces (RKHS), with applications to functional data. This project led to the submitted paper listed below.

Summary of my PhD

My PhD research was carried out in extreme value statistics, with a particular focus on the study and estimation of extreme quantiles of high-dimensional conditional distributions. It was structured around three main axes: (1) the construction of confidence intervals for extreme quantiles in general domains of attraction, (2) the development of an original dimension-reduction method for estimating the conditional tail index, and (3) a comparative study of modern approaches (machine learning, dimension reduction) for conditional extreme quantile estimation.
